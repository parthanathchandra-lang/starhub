Privacy, Safety & Terms

What StarHub actually does

StarHub is a directory of links to other services (YouTube, Play Store, Discord, etc.). It shows a name, an icon, and a link. When you click a tile, your browser opens that service directly. StarHub does not host, stream, mirror, or store any video, game, app file, or other media. Playback and downloads always happen on the original platform's own servers, under that platform's own terms.

What data StarHub collects

StarHub's own pages do not run visitor analytics, do not use tracking cookies, and do not ask for accounts, phone numbers, or SIM/carrier information. Any "Added" links you create are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to us.

Static hosting providers (Cloudflare, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Firebase, Surge) each process standard web-server logs to serve the page and to protect against abuse — this is normal for any website and is controlled by their own privacy policies, not by us. We do not have a database of our own, so we have nothing to sell, leak, or hand over beyond what those hosts log by default.

Can StarHub hide its server IP from everyone?

No — and no legitimate website can. A browser needs a real IP address to connect to any server, so the address is never truly invisible; what can be done is placing a reverse proxy (e.g. Cloudflare, in "proxied" mode) in front of the origin server, which hides the origin's IP from ordinary visitors while Cloudflare's own IP is publicly visible. Law enforcement or a court-ordered legal request can still compel any hosting provider, including a proxy provider, to disclose origin details. We will not claim otherwise, and treat any claim of "fully hidden, untraceable hosting" as false.

When would StarHub share information with anyone?

Only in response to a valid legal request from a competent authority (e.g. a court order or lawful government request), and only to the extent required by law. We do not sell data, and we do not share user information with advertisers or third parties for marketing.
